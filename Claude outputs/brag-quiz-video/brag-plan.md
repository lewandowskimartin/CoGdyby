# brag plan — Prepared at Home quiz (20s, 9:16)

**What it is:** a free 2-minute UK household preparedness quiz: 10 questions, a score out of 100, your top three gaps with a free fix for each.
**Who for:** UK households (mostly women 25–65 running the home) who know they "should" sort this out.
**What sets it apart:** based on the GOV.UK Prepare guidance, no sign-up, answers never stored, ends with specific fixes, not fear.
**Visual hook:** the question itself, big in Fraunces on cream: "How prepared is your household?"
**Tone:** `polished` pacing, brand voice: calm neighbour, sentence case, no red, no sirens, no clocks/countdowns, no exclamation marks.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.
