# Preparedness quiz: questions and answers

Generated from `src/data/quiz.js`, which the quiz reads. Edit the questions there, not here.

## How scoring works

- Each question has three answers worth **0, 1 or 2 points**.
- Score = total points × 5, out of 100 (10 questions × 2 points max = 20 points).
- **Top gaps:** up to three questions scored below 2, lowest points first, then by area priority (the order of the areas below), then by question order.
- **Weakest area:** the area with the lowest share of its possible points; ties go to the earlier area.

**Areas, in priority order:** 1. Your household plan · 2. Power and utilities · 3. Food and water · 4. Leaving in a hurry · 5. Warnings and neighbours
